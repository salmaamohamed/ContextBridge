ROLE: Talent Acquisition Manager / Lead
DEPARTMENT: Human Resources

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Formulate long-term hiring strategies aligned with company growth goals.
- Build university partnership programs for hiring fresh graduates and interns.
- Oversee employer branding, job postings, and campus recruitment drives.
- Analyze recruitment metrics to optimize hiring channels and pipeline efficiency.
- Partner with executive leadership to forecast headcount needs and department budgets.

REQUIRED SKILLS:
- Strategic workforce planning and recruitment analytics.
- Strong talent branding and partner relationship management.
- Expertise in technical hiring trends and market compensation benchmarks.
