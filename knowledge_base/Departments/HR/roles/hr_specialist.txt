ROLE: HR Specialist
DEPARTMENT: Human Resources

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Handle day-to-day HR administrative operations and employee inquiries.
- Coordinate the employee onboarding and offboarding workflows.
- Maintain accuracy in HR systems, employment contracts, and documentation.
- Assist in planning employee engagement initiatives and training programs.
- Serve as the primary point of contact for company policy clarifications.

REQUIRED SKILLS:
- Deep knowledge of local labor laws and HR regulations.
- Strong interpersonal, conflict resolution, and communication skills.
- Proficiency with HR software (HRIS), ATS, and communication tools.
