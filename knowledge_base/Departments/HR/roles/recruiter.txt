ROLE: Recruiter
DEPARTMENT: Human Resources

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Source qualified candidates through LinkedIn, job boards, and referral programs.
- Screen incoming resumes and conduct preliminary phone/video interviews.
- Schedule and coordinate technical interviews with hiring managers and team leads.
- Manage interview feedback and ensure a positive candidate experience.
- Maintain applicant pipelines across all open job requisitions.

REQUIRED SKILLS:
- Proven sourcing capabilities and active candidate outreach experience.
- Strong candidate assessment and communication skills.
- Familiarity with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
