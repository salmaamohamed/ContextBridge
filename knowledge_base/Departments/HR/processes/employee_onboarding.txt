PROCESS: Employee Onboarding Process

STEPS:
1. Pre-Onboarding (Day -7 to Day 0):
   - HR triggers ContextBridge system setup and sends welcome email.
   - Prepare workspace, equipment, email account, and permissions.
2. Day 1 - Orientation:
   - HR welcome session, policy review, and administrative paperwork.
   - Assign buddy/mentor and introduce to team.
3. Week 1 - Core Training & Personalization:
   - Employee completes ContextBridge personalized learning path based on role.
   - Initial check-in with HR Specialist and direct supervisor.
4. Month 1 to 3 - Integration & Assessment:
   - Complete department-specific simulations and tasks.
   - 30-60-90 day performance and onboarding check-ins.
