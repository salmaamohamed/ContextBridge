PROCESS: Recruitment Process

STEPS:
1. Requisition & Approval: Hiring manager submits requisition; HR approves budget & job description.
2. Sourcing & Posting: Recruiter posts job on career site, LinkedIn, and external boards.
3. Resume Screening: Recruiter reviews applicants against core requirements.
4. Initial Screening: Recruiter conducts 15-30 min culture & background call.
5. Technical/Department Assessment: Interview or practical test with team lead/senior.
6. Final Interview: Culture fit & alignment discussion with HR Lead or Executive.
7. Offer Proposal: Extended written offer, contract negotiation, and background check.
8. Transition to Onboarding: Candidate signs offer; handover to Employee Onboarding team.
