DEPARTMENT: Human Resources (HR)
OVERVIEW:
The Human Resources department manages the entire employee lifecycle at ContextBridge. 
This includes talent acquisition, onboarding, performance management, compliance, 
employee relations, and offboarding.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Sourcing and recruiting top technical and non-technical talent.
- Managing smooth onboarding experiences for fresh graduates, interns, and experienced hires.
- Maintaining employee records, legal compliance, and internal HR policies.
- Overseeing performance reviews, training programs, and career growth tracks.
- Handling offboarding, exit interviews, and documentation.

PRIMARY METRICS:
- Time-to-hire & Cost-per-hire
- Onboarding satisfaction score
- Early turnover rate (< 90 days)
- Employee retention & engagement score
