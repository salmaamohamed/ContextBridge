TOOL: HRIS (Human Resources Information System)
PURPOSE: Central database for managing employee profiles, payroll, leave requests, and documentation.

CORE FEATURES:
- Employee Profile Management (personal details, contract, salary history).
- Leave & Attendance Management (time-off requests, sick leave, public holidays).
- Performance Management Tracking.
- HR Document Storage (contracts, IDs, tax forms).

ACCESS PERMISSIONS:
- HR Staff: Full Read/Write access.
- Employees: Self-service portal access (View profile, request leaves, view payslips).
