TOOL: ATS (Applicant Tracking System)
PURPOSE: Pipeline management tool for job postings, applicant tracking, and hiring workflows.

CORE FEATURES:
- Candidate profile management & resume parsing.
- Automated email communication & interview scheduling.
- Interview evaluation forms & scorecards.
- Candidate pipeline analytics (time-to-hire, drop-off rate).

ACCESS PERMISSIONS:
- Recruiters & HR Specialists: Admin / Pipeline management access.
- Hiring Managers: Review candidate resumes and submit scorecards for specific roles.
