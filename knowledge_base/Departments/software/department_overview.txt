SOFTWARE ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT OVERVIEW

Company: NovaPay Technologies
Scope: Company
Category: department_overview
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-08-01

------------------------------------------------------------

1. MISSION
The Software Engineering department designs, builds, and maintains all customer-facing and internal systems that power NovaPay's payments platform, ensuring reliability, security, and scalability as the company grows.

2. TEAM STRUCTURE
The Software Engineering department currently has 42 employees, organized into the following sub-teams:
- Payments Platform (backend)
- Web & Mobile Clients (frontend)
- Platform Infrastructure
- Quality Engineering

Each sub-team has a lead who reports to the Head of Software Engineering, who in turn reports to the VP of Operations. New employees are assigned a mentor from their sub-team for the first 30 days.

3. HOW THIS DEPARTMENT WORKS
Work is tracked in Jira under the "SW" project key. Sprint planning happens every two weeks on Monday mornings, with a retrospective on the final Friday of each sprint. Daily standups are held at 9:30 AM and take no more than 15 minutes.

4. KEY TOOLS
- Git / GitHub
- Jira
- Docker & Kubernetes
- Datadog (monitoring)
- Slack

5. HOW SUCCESS IS MEASURED
- Sprint velocity and predictability
- Production incident rate (target: <2 sev-1 incidents/quarter)
- Code review turnaround time (target: <24h)
- Test coverage (target: >80% on new code)

6. WHO TO CONTACT
- Department questions: #sw-general on Slack
- Onboarding questions: your assigned mentor or the onboarding-support channel
- Escalations: department lead, then HR if unresolved within 3 business days

7. NEW EMPLOYEE EXPECTATIONS
New employees are expected to complete environment setup within the first 2 days, shadow at least one process end-to-end within the first week, and submit a first real contribution (ticket, report, or task) within the first two weeks. Readiness is assessed at the 30-day mark through a short review with the department lead and mentor.
