TITLE: Common Onboarding FAQs

TYPE: FAQ
CATEGORY: GENERAL_ONBOARDING
SCOPE: NEW_EMPLOYEES

Q: What should I do first during onboarding?

A:
Start with your assigned onboarding path. Review the relevant
company information, department information, project information,
and assigned onboarding tasks.

Q: Who determines my onboarding path?

A:
The onboarding path is based on your employee context, including
your department, role, project, and experience.

Q: Where should I look for company-specific information?

A:
Use the approved Company Knowledge Base.

Q: Where should I look for project-specific technical information?

A:
Use the relevant Project Knowledge Base.

Q: Where should I look if I have a common setup problem?

A:
Check the approved Recurring Problems and FAQs knowledge.

Q: What should I do if the documentation does not answer my question?

A:
If the question is generic technical knowledge, the general
technical support path may be used when applicable.

If the question requires company-specific or project-specific
information that is not available, escalate to the appropriate
senior employee with the relevant context.

Q: What information should I include when asking for help?

A:
Include:

- Your department
- Your role
- Your project
- The task you are working on
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Error messages
- What you already tried

Q: Should I follow generic internet instructions if they conflict
with company documentation?

A:
Approved internal company or project knowledge has priority for
company-specific processes and systems.

Q: Can I change project dependencies to fix an error?

A:
Do not arbitrarily change project dependencies. Follow the
project's approved dependency procedure or escalate the issue.

Q: What if I cannot access a required system?

A:
Check the Account Access and System Access troubleshooting
guidance. If access is still unavailable, use the approved
access request/escalation process.

Q: What if I do not know who can help me?

A:
Use the approved onboarding support or escalation process and
provide the relevant context about your question or blocker.

Q: Can the AI assistant invent an answer when company information
is missing?

A:
No. Company-specific and project-specific information should be
grounded in approved internal knowledge. If reliable approved
information is unavailable, the issue should be escalated.
