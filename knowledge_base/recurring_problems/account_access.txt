TITLE: Account Access Problems

TYPE: RECURRING_PROBLEM
CATEGORY: ACCOUNT_ACCESS
SCOPE: ALL_EMPLOYEES

PROBLEM:
Employee cannot access a required company account.

COMMON_SYMPTOMS:

- Account has not been created.
- Login credentials have not been received.
- Account exists but access is denied.
- Password reset is required.
- Required permissions are missing.

TROUBLESHOOTING:

1. Confirm that the employee's account should already exist.

2. Confirm that the correct company identity/account is being used.

3. Check whether the employee received the required invitation.

4. Verify whether the requested system requires additional approval.

5. Verify that the employee belongs to the correct department,
team, or project.

6. If access is still unavailable, escalate through the approved
access request process.

REQUIRED_ESCALATION_INFORMATION:

- Employee name/identifier
- Department
- Role
- Project
- System requiring access
- Current error message
- Date/time of access attempt

APPROVED_SOLUTION:
Follow the company's account provisioning and access request
process. Do not bypass access controls.
