TITLE: Communication and Escalation Process

TYPE: GENERAL_PROCESS
CATEGORY: COMMUNICATION
SCOPE: ALL_EMPLOYEES

PURPOSE:
Define how employees should handle questions, blockers,
and issues during onboarding and regular work.

PROCESS:

1. Identify the question or problem.

2. Check the relevant approved documentation.

3. Check project-specific documentation when applicable.

4. Check approved recurring problems and FAQs.

5. If the issue is technical and generic, use the approved
general technical support path when available.

6. If the issue remains unresolved, contact the appropriate
team member or senior employee.

7. Include sufficient context when escalating.

ESCALATION_CONTEXT:

An escalation should include:

- Original question/problem
- Employee department
- Employee role
- Project
- Relevant task
- What has already been attempted
- Relevant error message
- Reason the issue could not be resolved

PRINCIPLE:

Approved internal knowledge has priority over generic information.

Company-specific or project-specific information must not be
invented when it is unavailable.
