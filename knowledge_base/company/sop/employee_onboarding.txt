TITLE: Technical Employee Onboarding SOP

TYPE: SOP
CATEGORY: EMPLOYEE_ONBOARDING
SCOPE: NEW_EMPLOYEES

PURPOSE:
Provide a standard process for onboarding employees into their
department, role, and project.

ONBOARDING_SEQUENCE:

1. Create Employee Profile
2. Assign Department
3. Assign Role
4. Assign Project
5. Review Company Information
6. Provide Required Access
7. Set Up Development/Work Environment
8. Review Department Processes
9. Review Project Knowledge
10. Assign Onboarding Tasks
11. Complete Practical Work
12. Complete Assessment
13. Review Progress and Skill Gaps
14. Confirm Work Readiness

EMPLOYEE_PROFILE:
The onboarding profile should contain:

- Department
- Role
- Project
- Experience

PERSONALIZED_PATH:
The onboarding path should be based on the employee's context.

The path may include:

- Company processes
- Product/project knowledge
- Professional expectations
- Technology stack
- Tools
- Onboarding tasks

SUPPORT:
Employees can use the onboarding support process when they
encounter problems or have questions.

ESCALATION:
If approved company or project knowledge cannot provide a reliable
answer, the issue should be escalated to the appropriate senior
employee or supervisor.

COMPLETION:
Onboarding completion should consider:

- Completed learning
- Completed tasks
- Assessment results
- Identified skill gaps
- Remaining blockers
