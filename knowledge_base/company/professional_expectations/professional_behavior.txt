TITLE: Professional Expectations

TYPE: PROFESSIONAL_EXPECTATIONS
CATEGORY: WORKPLACE_BEHAVIOR
SCOPE: ALL_EMPLOYEES

PURPOSE:
Define general professional expectations for employees.

EXPECTATIONS:

1. Employees should communicate clearly with their team.

2. Employees should keep relevant stakeholders informed about
progress and blockers.

3. Employees should ask for clarification when requirements are
unclear.

4. Employees should document important technical or operational
information.

5. Employees should follow agreed team processes.

6. Employees should complete assigned work within agreed
timeframes or communicate delays early.

7. Employees should participate appropriately in code reviews,
technical discussions, meetings, and team activities relevant
to their role.

8. Employees should accept constructive feedback and use it
to improve their work.

9. Employees should raise blockers rather than remaining blocked
without communication.

10. Employees should distinguish between confirmed company
information and assumptions.

ONBOARDING_EXPECTATION:

A new employee is expected to:

- Learn the relevant company processes.
- Understand their department responsibilities.
- Understand their project context.
- Complete assigned onboarding tasks.
- Ask questions when information is unclear.
- Communicate blockers.
- Demonstrate practical understanding.
