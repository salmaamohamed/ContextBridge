TITLE: Remote Work Policy

TYPE: COMPANY_POLICY
CATEGORY: REMOTE_WORK
SCOPE: ALL_EMPLOYEES

PURPOSE:
Define general expectations for employees working remotely.

POLICY:

1. Employees must use approved company communication and
collaboration tools.

2. Employees must maintain access to required company systems
during their working period.

3. Company information must be protected when working outside
company facilities.

4. Employees should avoid accessing confidential company
information through unsecured networks.

5. Company devices and approved security controls must be used
according to company requirements.

6. Work progress, blockers, and important issues should be
communicated through the appropriate team channels.

7. Employees must follow their team's working hours and
communication expectations.

ESCALATION:
If an employee cannot access a required system or tool while
working remotely, the employee should follow the approved
access/support process.
