TITLE: Acceptable Use Policy

TYPE: COMPANY_POLICY
CATEGORY: ACCEPTABLE_USE
SCOPE: ALL_EMPLOYEES

PURPOSE:
Define the acceptable use of company systems, accounts, devices,
networks, applications, and information.

POLICY:

1. Company systems must be used for authorized business purposes.

2. Employees must use only accounts and permissions assigned to them.

3. Employees must not share passwords, authentication tokens,
API keys, access credentials, or private keys with other employees
through unauthorized channels.

4. Employees must not attempt to access systems, repositories,
databases, files, or services without authorization.

5. Company data must not be copied to personal storage or
unauthorized external services.

6. Employees must not install unauthorized software on company
devices or development environments.

7. Employees must follow the company's security procedures when
handling confidential or restricted information.

8. Security incidents, suspicious activity, or accidental exposure
of sensitive information must be reported through the approved
company process.

9. Employees must follow the access permissions associated with
their role and project.

10. When an employee changes teams, projects, or responsibilities,
access must be reviewed and updated according to company procedures.

APPROVED_BEHAVIOR:
- Use authorized accounts.
- Use approved tools.
- Follow assigned permissions.
- Protect company information.
- Report security concerns.

PROHIBITED_BEHAVIOR:
- Credential sharing.
- Unauthorized system access.
- Unauthorized data transfer.
- Circumventing security controls.
- Installing unapproved software.

ESCALATION:
Questions about acceptable use that are not covered by this policy
should be escalated to the appropriate company administrator,
security contact, or supervisor.
